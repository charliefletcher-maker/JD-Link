The problem

An AlphaFold, but for how people think.

Give 1,000 people a for-or-against position on 10 issues and there are 103,000 possible group opinion states. DeepMind only has to model 10300 ways a protein can fold. Searching that space fast enough for a client to get an answer in hours, not months, is a frontier ML problem.
